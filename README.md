# 📖 About The Project

Livestock diseases are one of the major reasons for reduced milk production, economic losses, and poor animal health worldwide. Early diagnosis is often difficult because veterinary services may not always be immediately available, especially in rural areas.

The **AI-Powered Cattle Disease Detection System** provides a fast and intelligent solution by using **Computer Vision** and **Deep Learning** to automatically identify cattle diseases from images.

The system can classify cattle into:

- 🦠 Foot-and-Mouth Disease (FMD)
- 🐄 Healthy
- 🔴 Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD)

The goal is to assist farmers with **instant preliminary diagnosis**, enabling timely veterinary consultation and reducing disease spread.

# 🦠 Diseases Detected

| Disease | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| 🦠 Foot-and-Mouth Disease | Highly contagious viral disease causing fever, mouth blisters and lameness. |
| 🔴 Lumpy Skin Disease | Viral disease producing nodules, skin lesions and productivity loss. |
| 🟢 Healthy | Indicates cattle showing no visible disease symptoms. |
